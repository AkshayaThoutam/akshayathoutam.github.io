Learning Biologically Meaningful Representations

Foundation models, representation learning, large-scale transcriptomic embeddings

×

Machine learning methods are increasingly used to model complex biological systems, particularly in the analysis of large and multimodal datasets. However, models that achieve strong predictive performance do not always learn representations that are biologically meaningful or interpretable. I develop models that learn representations reflecting both accurate prediction and deeper biological understanding.

Causal Modeling of Disease Programs

Causal inference, perturbation analysis, counterfactual reasoning, structural modeling, identifiability

×

Transcriptomic analyses of disease are typically dominated by correlational patterns that do not resolve causal structure. Causal modeling frameworks enable identification of molecular drivers rather than downstream consequences. I apply these methods to disentangle causal mechanisms underlying cell states and disease progression from confounded or spurious associations.

Biophysics of Nanoplasmonics

Nanotechnology, biophysics, and physical principles of biological systems

×

Biological systems are governed not only by molecular interactions but also by fundamental physical principles operating at the nanoscale. I use nanotechnology and biophysical modeling to understand how physical properties—size, structure, and force—shape molecular organization and behavior. By studying nanoparticles as engineered probes of biological systems, this work connects physical design with biological function.

Clinical Translation and Responsible AI

Bioethics, responsible AI, societal impact of ML in healthcare

×

The integration of machine learning into clinical research introduces ethical challenges that extend beyond technical performance, including concerns around bias, transparency, patient representation, and downstream impact on medical decision-making. I focus on embedding principles of responsible AI and bioethics into the design and evaluation of computational models for biomedical research. Aligning methodological rigor with ethical accountability ensures that AI-driven discoveries are both scientifically valid and clinically trustworthy.
